# Platinum Overwatch: P10-Vector
**Open-Source Situational Awareness & Medical Logistics Dashboard**

## The Mission: Securing the Platinum Ten

In trauma medicine and executive protection, the "Platinum Ten" minutes are the critical interval for stabilizing life-threatening injuries. Platinum Overwatch is a tactical dashboard that fuses open-source intelligence (OSINT) with live environmental data to ensure dispatchers and detail leaders can visualize and bypass logistical threats before they impact medical extraction.

## Operational Capabilities

- **Live Threat Fusion:** Aggregates real-time data from GDACS (Global Disasters) and PredictHQ (Civil Unrest/Politics/Severe Weather).
- **Dynamic Filtering:** iOS-style segmented controls to instantly isolate Medical/Rescue alerts from Civil/Threat intelligence.
- **Geofenced Threat Radius:** One-tap GPS localization that draws a 10-mile operational perimeter around the command post or principal.
- **NEXRAD Weather Overlay:** Live, animated US National Weather Service radar to identify severe storm cells impacting extraction routes.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React, Leaflet.js, React-Leaflet
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Intelligence APIs:** GDACS, PredictHQ, Iowa Environmental Mesonet (NWS Radar)
- **UI/UX:** Apple Dark Mode Enterprise Interface
